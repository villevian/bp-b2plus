BInvent a new feature for one of the apps. Write 5–7 survey questions to find out what users feel and what they’d like.

Mix question types: at least one open, one closed (yes/no), one rating (1–10) and one multiple-choice question. Start general, then get more specific.

CYour teacher is the respondent. Ask your questions and take notes.

DPresent your feature in two minutes: what you learnt from the answers and what you’ll change. Your teacher says if their needs were taken into account.

According to the survey, …The respondent said / suggested that …Based on the answers, we’ll …We decided not to … because …

★Instead of a survey, run a five-minute user interview with your teacher. Follow three rules.

Their life, not your ideaDon’t pitch the feature. Ask how they do things now.
The past, not the future“Tell me about the last time you …” — not “Would you use …?”
Listen moreTalk 20%, listen 80%. Ask “Why?” and “What happened next?”
Compliments are not data“Great idea!” tells you nothing. Ask what they actually did.
✗ Would you use an app that …?✓ How do you … at the moment?✗ Do you like our idea?✓ What’s the hardest part about …?✓ What have you tried so far?✓ Is there anything else I should have asked?
